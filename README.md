# E-Commerce Orders & Sales Dashboard

## 1. Project Overview
The E-Commerce Orders & Sales Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project created using **IBM Cognos Analytics**. It helps analyze order details, sales performance, product categories, city-wise orders, courier performance, and order status.

The main objective of this dashboard is to convert raw e-commerce data into meaningful visual insights that support better business decisions.

## 2. Objectives
- Monitor total orders and overall sales.
- Analyze delivered and returned orders.
- Compare orders across different cities.
- Evaluate courier-wise order distribution.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Understand the distribution of order statuses.

## 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Dashboard Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Orders | 1.2K |
| Total Sales | ₹14.81 Million |
| Returned Orders | 26 |
| Delivered Orders | 1.041K |

## 4. Charts and Visualizations

**1. Orders by City — Horizontal Bar Chart**

Displays the number of orders across cities such as Nagpur, Indore, Patna, Bhopal, Kanpur, Jaipur, Lucknow, and Surat. It helps compare order volumes by location.

**2. Orders by Courier — Horizontal Bar Chart**

Compares order counts handled by BlueRoute, SwiftShip, QuickKart, and ParcelPro. It helps understand the distribution of orders among courier services.

**3. Sales by Product Category — Horizontal Bar Chart**

Shows sales revenue for Fashion, Electronics, Home & Kitchen, Beauty, Sports, and Books. Fashion appears to be the highest-selling category in the displayed chart.

**4. Order Value Trend — Line Chart**

Displays order values against Order IDs. It shows a generally decreasing trend in the displayed values. However, Order ID is an identifier rather than a date, so a date-based line chart would be more suitable for analyzing sales trends over time.

**5. Order Status — Doughnut Chart**

Shows the distribution of orders by status: Delivered, Delayed, Cancelled, and Returned. Delivered orders represent the largest share of the displayed order statuses.

## 5. Tools and Technologies
- **IBM Cognos Analytics:** Dashboard creation and data visualization.
- **E-commerce Dataset:** Source data for order, sales, product, city, courier, and status analysis.
- **Charts and KPIs:** Used to present business information clearly.

## 6. Key Insights
- The dashboard records approximately 1.2K total orders.
- Total order value is approximately ₹14.81 million.
- Delivered orders account for the majority of orders.
- Fashion generates the highest sales among the displayed product categories.
- Nagpur has the highest order count among the cities shown.
- BlueRoute handles the highest order count among the displayed courier services.
- Returned orders total 26.

## 7. Benefits of the Dashboard
- Provides a quick overview of business performance.
- Makes sales and order comparisons easier.
- Helps identify high-performing cities and product categories.
- Supports order fulfilment and courier performance analysis.
- Presents important business metrics in one place.

## 8. Conclusion
The E-Commerce Orders & Sales Dashboard provides a clear visual summary of order and sales performance. By combining KPIs, bar charts, a line chart, and a doughnut chart, it helps users understand business operations and identify areas for improvement. This project demonstrates the practical use of IBM Cognos Analytics for business intelligence and data visualization.
